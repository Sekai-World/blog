## Progress of Sekai Viewer Reborn

In the [previous post](/blog/looking-ahead-sekai-viewer-reborn-and-migration-plan), I shared the long-term plan to gradually move from the legacy Sekai Viewer to **Sekai Viewer Reborn**. Here is where things stand now.

Development has kept moving steadily. The content site, which is the first public-facing part of Reborn, continues to grow, with pages for game information such as cards, music, characters and events becoming more complete and more polished.

Reborn is built on the ideas I described when the project was first announced: smaller, lighter server-side rendered applications instead of one huge single-page app. The practical benefits are:

- **Faster pages** that are easier to load and easier to keep fast as the site grows.
- **Shareable links** that show a correct preview on social media, and pages that search engines can actually see.
- **A maintainable foundation**, so individual pages and tools can be improved one at a time instead of waiting for one very large update.

## The preview is coming soon

The biggest news: **a public preview of Sekai Viewer Reborn will be available soon.**

To be clear about what to expect: it is a *preview*. Some things will be rough, some features from the current Sekai Viewer will not be there yet, and you may run into bugs. That is exactly why we want you to try it. Your feedback will help decide what gets polished and what gets built next.

I'm not announcing an exact date today, because I would rather release it when it is ready than rush it out. When it is live, I will announce it here and on [@SekaiViewer](https://twitter.com/SekaiViewer) with the link and instructions on how to send feedback.

In the meantime, the current Sekai Viewer stays available as usual. You do not need to change anything.

## Want to help?

Reborn is still a community project built in limited time, and help is always welcome. Whether you can contribute on the technical side, or with web design, translation or user stories, please feel free to contact [@SekaiViewer](https://twitter.com/SekaiViewer) or write an email to dev@sekai.best.

Thank you for six wonderful years of Project Sekai, and for being part of Sekai Viewer. Here's to many more!
